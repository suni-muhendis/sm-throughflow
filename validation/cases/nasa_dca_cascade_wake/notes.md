# NASA CR-185118 low-speed cascade wake

Zierke and Deutsch measured the far wake of the middle DCA cascade blade.
Volume II, Table 80 gives streamwise speed and deviations at 152.6% chord
and -8.5 degrees incidence. The full measured interval is 59.945 mm. It
does not contain a complete projected pitch. Volume I, printed p. 12 gives
chord 228.6 mm and solidity 2.14. Table 2 gives outlet angle -0.6 degrees,
inlet speed 33.28 m/s and Reynolds number 507,000 for this incidence.

Table 2 distinguishes a measured blade-passage loss coefficient of 0.028
from the authors' velocity-profile reductions of 0.024 at 131.9% chord and
0.018 at 152.6% chord. Volume I, printed p. 85 describes the constant
static-pressure approximation and separate edge references on each side
of the wake. The survey-plane calculation compares to the 0.018 reduction.
The 0.028 probe result and the computed mixed-out coefficient are separate
quantities.

The test exercises wake data reduction in a low-speed compressor cascade.
It does not supply a transonic blade pressure distribution, direct entropy
data or a measured Stage 35–38 blockage correction.
