# Stage 8-8 (NASA TM X-2926) transcription notes

Source: Osborn, Urasek, Moore, NASA TM X-2926 (November 1973), NTRS
19740002621, file `TMX2926_sol15.pdf`. "The stage is designated stage 8-8
(rotor 8, stator 8)" (pdf p4). Tip solidity 1.5 member of the solidity series
(1.3 = stage 14-10, TM X-2645; 1.7 = stage 12-5, TM X-2658).

## Uncertain cells / inconsistencies

None marked '?'. All rows pass the triangle, Mach, U = omega r (16100 rpm),
camber = KIC - KOC and solidity (N = 49 / 54) checks.

**Printed anomaly, transcribed as printed:** Table V (stator 8) axial
dimensions at RP 3 and RP 4 break the smooth spanwise trend of every other
column: ZIC 7.531 / 7.523, ZMC 9.393 / 9.381, ZOC 11.502 / 11.491, whereas
the neighbouring rows are ZIC 7.61-7.64, ZMC 9.45-9.49, ZOC 11.57-11.60. The
digits are clear in the image (re-read at 2.4x). These two rows look shifted
about 0.09 cm upstream (possibly a printout artefact or a real stacking
result); a later interpretation step should treat them with care.

## Text facts

- 49 rotor / 54 stator blades, aspect ratio 2.4 / 2.0, MCA both rows.
- Damper at about 48 % span from the outlet rotor tip, max thickness
  0.214 cm (no further geometry; figure 3 photograph only).
- Nominal nonrotating tip clearance 0.050 cm at ambient conditions; no
  running clearance given.
- Rotor hub TE to stator hub LE axial spacing 3.33 cm (stator hub ZIC 7.628 -
  rotor hub ZOC 4.295 = 3.333, consistent with the Z columns sharing the
  rotor-hub-LE origin of Figure 1; interpretation, not stated).
- Station 3 traverse one stator passage (6.7 deg).

## Conventions

As in TM X-2645 / X-2658: beta from the axial direction; KIC/KTC/KOC from the
meridional plane; PERCENT SPAN from the tip at rotor outlet; sections on
conical surfaces approximating stream surfaces stacked through the centres of
gravity; Z referenced from the rotor-blade-hub leading edge; ZIC.. defined as
"axial distance ... from inlet".
